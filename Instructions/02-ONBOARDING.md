# BudgetPlan — Onboarding UX

## Flow
```text
Splash
→ Welcome
→ Currency
→ Income Setup
→ Budget Method
→ Categories
→ Financial Goal
→ Dashboard
```

## Screens

### Splash
Show logo, app name, short tagline.

### Welcome
Headline: Take control of your money.
CTA: Get Started.

### Currency
Default to PHP for Philippine users but support multiple currencies.

### Income Setup
Collect:
- Income amount
- Frequency
- Source
- Multiple income sources

Supported frequencies:
- Weekly
- Bi-weekly
- Monthly
- Irregular

### Budget Method
Options:
- 50/30/20
- Zero-Based Budget
- Custom Budget

### Categories
Provide defaults for:
- Housing
- Food
- Utilities
- Transportation
- Healthcare
- Shopping
- Entertainment
- Savings
- Investments
- Debt

Allow custom categories.

### Financial Goal
Optional:
- Emergency Fund
- House
- Car
- Travel
- Gadget
- Education
- Wedding
- Custom

After onboarding, open Home Dashboard.
