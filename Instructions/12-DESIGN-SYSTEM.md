# BudgetPlan — UI Design System

## Visual Direction
Style:
- Clean
- Minimal
- Modern
- Trustworthy
- Finance-focused
- Accessible

## Suggested Colors
```text
Primary: #2563EB
Success: #16A34A
Warning: #F59E0B
Danger: #DC2626
Background: #F8FAFC
Card: #FFFFFF
Text: #0F172A
Secondary Text: #64748B
```

## Dark Mode
```text
Background: #0F172A
Card: #1E293B
```

## Typography
Recommended:
- Inter
- Plus Jakarta Sans

Hierarchy:
- H1: 28–32px bold
- H2: 22–24px bold
- H3: 18–20px semibold
- Body: 14–16px
- Caption: 12–13px

## Cards
- Radius: 12–16px
- Padding: 16–20px
- Subtle shadow
- Avoid excessive cards

## Buttons
Primary, secondary and destructive variants.

## Accessibility
- Maintain readable contrast
- Do not communicate status through color alone
- Use icons + labels
- Ensure touch targets are large enough
- Provide clear focus states

## Empty States
Always explain what happened and provide the next action.

## Error States
Use friendly user-facing messages, never raw database/server errors.
