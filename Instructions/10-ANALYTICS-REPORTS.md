# BudgetPlan — Analytics and Reports

## Analytics
Provide:
- Spending over time
- Income over time
- Category breakdown
- Savings rate
- Budget usage
- Month-to-month comparison

## Charts
Recommended:
- Line chart for monthly spending
- Donut chart for category distribution
- Bar chart for income vs expenses
- Progress bars for budgets and goals

## Financial Insights
Examples:
- Spending increased compared with previous month.
- Food budget is close to its limit.
- Savings rate improved.
- User is on track for a goal.

## Monthly Report
Show:
- Income
- Expenses
- Savings
- Remaining
- Savings rate
- Top spending categories
- Budget performance
- Goal progress

## Export
Future support:
- CSV
- PDF
