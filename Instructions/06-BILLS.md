# BudgetPlan — Bills Module

## Purpose
Bills are recurring or scheduled financial obligations and should be distinct from ordinary expenses.

## Bill List
Sections:
- Overdue
- Upcoming
- Paid

## Bill Card
Show:
- Bill name
- Amount
- Due date
- Category
- Status

## Bill Detail
Fields:
- Name
- Amount
- Category
- Due date
- Recurring
- Frequency
- Status

Actions:
- Mark as Paid
- Edit
- Delete

## Reminder Logic
Examples:
- 7 days before due date
- 3 days before due date
- 1 day before due date
- Due today
- Overdue

Allow users to configure reminder timing.
